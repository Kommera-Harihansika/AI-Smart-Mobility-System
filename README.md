# AI Smart Mobility System 🚦🤖

![Smart Mobility System](smart_mobility_city.png)

## Overview

AI Smart Mobility System is a futuristic smart-city transportation platform that uses Artificial Intelligence and Gemini-powered assistance to reduce traffic congestion, improve emergency response, and provide efficient route recommendations.

---

## Problem Statement

Modern cities face several transportation challenges:

- Traffic congestion causes delays and productivity loss.
- Emergency vehicles get stuck in traffic.
- Traffic signals operate inefficiently.
- Citizens lack real-time intelligent travel guidance.
- Increasing vehicle usage contributes to pollution and fuel wastage.

As cities continue to grow, traditional transportation systems become less effective.

---

## Proposed Solution

The AI Smart Mobility System introduces an intelligent transportation network powered by AI and Gemini.

### 🚦 Smart Traffic Prediction

Uses machine learning models to predict traffic congestion based on:

- Time
- Location
- Weather conditions
- Historical traffic patterns

### 🗺 Route Optimization

Suggests the fastest and most efficient route to users.

### 🚑 Emergency Vehicle Priority

Detects emergency vehicles and automatically creates optimized routes.

### 🤖 Gemini Travel Assistant

Users can interact with Gemini through natural language.

**Example:**

> "How can I reach college before 9 AM?"

Gemini provides:

- Best route
- Expected travel time
- Traffic alerts
- Alternative routes

### 📊 Traffic Analytics Dashboard

Provides:

- Congestion levels
- Peak traffic hours
- Traffic trends
- Road condition reports
